# Pongso no Tao｜蘭嶼文化島 0.1.5（草稿）

> 0.1.5 分階段做。第一階段是根據試玩回饋修正的 9 項，第二階段是新的抵達開場，第三階段是新的角色選擇畫面和兩位族人分開選部落。之後的階段完成後，會再把內容加進這份說明。

## 第一階段：試玩回饋修正

**語言設定**
- 對話框裡不再出現語言按鈕。
- 開頭畫面可以選語言（雙語／僅繁中／僅族語），之後要改，到手札「存檔」頁的「設定」，就在音樂、音效旁邊。
- 換了馬上生效，下次打開遊戲也會記得。

**開場**
- 新遊戲改由男族人帶頭。

**鏡頭**
- 預設視野拉遠一點（約 14%），看得到更多周圍。
- 雙指縮放和「重設視角」都會回到新的預設距離。

**建築物不再被樹擋住**
- 清掉擋在建築物前面和門口的樹，例如蘭嶼氣象站。
- 每次發佈前都會自動檢查，有樹擋住建築物就會被抓出來。

**環島公路比較平順**
- 公路和路肩修平一些，走起來不會一直上上下下。
- 沒有新的斷崖，也沒有路面泡水。

**提醒**
- 提醒框移到右上角，文字變短，不會擋到角色。
- 拿到東西（例如「食材＋1」）改成在角色頭上冒出一行小字，然後淡出；不用等其他提醒關掉才出現。

**村民**
- 修好村民卡在路邊一直朝同一個方向走、或卡在房子裡的問題（例如野銀）。
- 村民現在在自己的地方附近走動，比較好找。
- 在背景走動的村民，走路速度和腳步都放慢到主角的三分之一。隊伍裡的夥伴還是跟主角走得一樣快。

**山羊**
- 山羊變成一小群一小群，會吃草、慢慢走動。
- 大多是黑山羊，偶爾有幾隻咖啡色或白色。

**港邊的遊艇**
- 停在港邊的遊艇，船尾改成白色；深藍色的線條只在兩側，到船尾轉角就停，跟開場插畫一樣。

**其他**
- 開頭畫面和手札設定裡會顯示目前的版本。

## 第二階段：新的抵達開場

**選交通方式之前**
- 和以前一樣，只看到同一個角度的蘭嶼。

**搭飛機**
- 鏡頭往後拉，看到機窗和機翼。等島嶼載入時，飛機輕輕晃，雲慢慢飄。
- 載入完成後，鏡頭穿出機窗，機翼往後退到鏡頭後面，只剩下蘭嶼。島換成更近的新角度。
- 接著小飛機從鏡頭後面飛出來，畫一道弧線：先看到機尾，再轉彎，最後往下朝蘭嶼降落。螺旋槳會轉，翼尖有白色氣流。
- 畫面淡出後打開手札，「抵達蘭嶼」蓋章完成，遊戲開始。

**搭船**
- 鏡頭往後拉，看到船頭。等島嶼載入時，海浪輕輕起伏，船頭跟著上下晃，雲慢慢飄。
- 載入完成後，換成從船尾看遊艇。船身自己左右搖，船尾的浪花和水花跟著速度變多，船越開越遠、越來越小，停在畫面左邊靠近島的地方。
- 同時蘭嶼換成更近的新角度，慢慢變大。畫面淡出後打開手札，和搭飛機一樣。

**手機**
- 直拿和橫拿手機時，機窗的框和船頭都完整出現，不會被切掉。
- 載入進度條移到畫面中間，半透明，不會擋住機窗或船頭。
- 修好開頭選家鄉的畫面：「踏上 Pongso no Tao」按鈕不再蓋住男生／女生按鈕。

**其他**
- 開場圖檔重新壓縮，換成新的一組，總共約 2 MB。舊的、用不到的圖已移除。

## 第三階段：新的角色選擇畫面，兩位族人可以來自不同部落

**角色選擇畫面**
- 三個人的頭像並排放在畫面正中間：男族人、女族人、旅人，下面各有一句話說明這趟回來想做什麼。
- 上面一句話說明：三個人一起旅行，各有各的目的。
- 男族人和女族人固定，不用選。旅人點一下頭像就能換男生或女生，也可以按頭像下面的「男生／女生」。
- 部落改成小的下拉選單，男族人、女族人各一個，預設都是「隨機」。兩個人可以選不同部落，也可以一樣。
- 選「隨機」時，兩個人各自抽，可能抽到同一個部落。
- 電腦、手機直拿、手機橫拿都看得到整個畫面，「踏上 Pongso no Tao」按鈕不會蓋住頭像或選單。

**兩位族人來自不同部落時**
- 大家住在男族人的家，這裡叫「主人家」。全隊一起做的事，例如聽島上的規矩、全家吃飯、在涼亭集合，都在主人家。
- 「回家報到」變成兩段：男族人、女族人各自回自己的部落，向家裡的長輩報到（點左上角頭像切換隊員），再一起到主人家的涼亭。
- 長輩的話也改了，不會再說兩個人是在同一個家長大的。
- 女性長輩交代的事（芋田、織布、曬魚、煮飯）在女族人的家；男性長輩交代的事（造船、出海）在男族人的家。只有一位族人參加的任務，就在那一位的家。
- 「尋根」：男族人問阿公的事、女族人問外婆的事，各自回自己的家拿衣服。
- 如果要拜訪的長輩剛好就是自己家的長輩，就改去問別村的長輩，不會同一個人講兩段不一樣的話。「把芋頭分給三戶親族」也一樣，會改送別村的親戚。
- 繼續遊戲那一行，兩個人不同部落時會寫「家鄉：椰油／野銀」。

**舊存檔**
- 以前的存檔照樣可以讀。讀進來時，兩位族人都算同一個部落，跟以前一樣。

**其他**
- 每次發佈前會自動檢查：六個部落的每一種組合，任務都找得到人和地點，也不會有同一位長輩被拜訪兩次。

## 第四階段：更大的島、地下屋的平台、每棟房子都有小路、中橫公路、地圖傳送

**島變大了**
- 整座島放大約四成。地形、環島公路、部落、景點、任務地點、出生點、族人和羊群活動的範圍、小地圖和鏡頭範圍，全都一起放大。
- 部落之間離得更遠。環島公路從大約三百公尺變成大約四百六十公尺。
- 紅頭現在在真實的位置，就在漁人旁邊。所以南邊這兩個部落還是比較近，但從紅頭到野銀的環島路變得很長，可以改走中橫公路。

**地下屋不再浮在坡上**
- 每一棟房子、工作屋、涼亭、教堂和學校，都蓋在一塊整平的平台上。平台在山坡上挖出來，靠山那邊有一小段土坎。
- 房子之間的距離拉開了，任兩棟房子之間至少留一公尺多，可以走路通過。
- 蓋在公路靠海那一側的房子，現在門會朝向公路。

**每棟房子都有小路**
- 每一棟房子（尤其是任務要去的雜貨店、學校、衛生所、機場、氣象站、燈塔）都有一條小路，從門口接到公路，或接到旁邊房子的小路。
- 小路是土色鋪石的，走起來平順。樹和花草不會長在小路上。
- 每次發佈前會自動檢查：每棟房子都有小路，小路都接得到公路，路上沒有東西擋住，也沒有太陡的地方。
- 任何建築都不會被樹擋住。

**中橫公路**
- 新增從紅頭到野銀、翻過山的中橫公路，大致照真實路線走，經過山頂的氣象站。
- 坡度和緩，路面平順，跟環島公路接在一起的地方也沒有落差。氣象站前面整平了一塊空地，從公路走得過去。

**地圖傳送**
- 「環島傳送」現在打開一張大地圖。在地圖上點一下地點就會過去：六個部落、開元港、機場、衛生所和青青草原。原本的按鈕清單拿掉了。
- 地圖上有紅色箭頭標出你現在的位置。
- 電腦、手機直拿和橫拿都能完整看到整張地圖。

**舊存檔**
- 0.1.4 的存檔照樣可以讀。讀進來時，每個人會被放到同一個部落或地點附近的公路上。

## 第五階段：英文版

**可以選英文了**
- 開始畫面上方（音樂、音效旁邊）和手札「存檔 → 設定」裡，多了一排「語言：中文／English」。
- 選了之後會記住，下次打開還是同一種語言。切換時畫面會重新整理一次，進度會先存好。
- 以前玩過的人、舊的存檔，沒有選過就還是中文，跟以前一樣。
- 原本的「雙語／僅繁中／僅族語」還在，改名叫「對話」。英文介面時顯示成「Tao + English／English only／Tao only」：族語加英文、只有英文、只有族語。

**翻成英文的內容**
- 所有任務、對話、物品、食譜、族語詞卡、歌謠說明、成就、操作說明，全部都有英文，一共約三千兩百段文字。
- 標題畫面、角色選擇、抵達選擇、畫面上的按鈕、提示訊息、手札每一頁、存檔和設定，也都是英文。
- 族語的詞維持族語（例如 akokey、soli、tatala），中文有解釋的地方，英文也附上解釋。
- 部落用族語名稱：Yayo、Iraraley、Iranmeylek、Ivalino、Imorod、Iratay。
- 「教學草稿」「還要請族人確認」這類提醒，英文版一樣保留。
- 用字整理在 docs/en-glossary.md，以後新增內容照這份用詞。

**英文放得下**
- 英文比中文長。電腦、手機直拿、手機橫拿都檢查過：按鈕、分頁、手札清單、設定都不會被切掉或跑出畫面。
- 手機上，上方的歲時按鈕在英文時用短的名稱（例如 Fish-calling、Flying fish），完整名稱在提示裡。

**地名也是英文了**（地圖階段做完後補上）
- 島上飄著的地名（部落、開元港、機場、衛生所、紅頭岩這些景點）、環島傳送地圖上的點、任務裡提到的地點，英文介面時全部是英文。
- 部落用族語名稱，所以地名下面不再重複一行族語。其他地方照用詞表：開元港 Kaiyuan Harbour、紅頭岩 Hongtou Rock、青青草原 Qingqing Prairie……
- 「外婆」在英文裡統一寫成 Grandma。

**中英並列與新字型**
- 語言多了第三個選項「中文＋English」。按鈕上的字永遠同時寫中文和英文，不會被翻掉。
- 沒選過語言的人，會照瀏覽器的語言決定：中文瀏覽器用中文；英文或其他語言的瀏覽器，預設中文＋English。
- 中文＋English 時，對話和提示訊息的中文稍微小一點，下面多一行小很多的英文。只選一種語言時，字的大小跟原本一樣。
- 換上新字型「霞光書卷」：中文的標題、內文和手札用霞鶩文楷，英文和族語用 Cormorant Garamond。英文字型本來比較小、比較細，所以英文的對話、提示和手札放大一點、加粗一點。字型檔也從約 1.9 MB 降到約 1 MB。
- 族語的詞在任何地方、任何語言模式下都不用斜體。
- 對話與提示的語氣順過一輪：口語、輕鬆，但不隨便；用詞表裡的固定譯名不變。

**檢查**
- 每次發佈前會自動檢查：每一段給玩家看的內容都有英文（地名也算）、英文裡沒有留下中文、名字和數字的位置沒有漏掉。英文畫面上只要出現一個中文字就算不通過。
- 也會檢查程式裡每一句介面中文都翻得出來，並在三種畫面大小上確認英文沒有超出框框。

## 第六階段：卡片與圖鑑手札

**第一次學到就有一張卡**
- 第一次學會一個族語詞、拿到一樣東西、學會一道菜，或做好一套衣服時，畫面中間會跳出一張明信片。
- 明信片的底是簡單的達悟紋樣；左邊是圖，右邊寫族語名稱，下面是中文、英文或中英兩行（照語言設定）。
- 一次學到三個以上的族語詞時，合成一張清單卡，不會連跳好幾張。
- 同時有好幾張卡時，一張看完才出下一張，不會疊在一起。
- 點一下卡片，它會縮小飛進右上角的「圖鑑手札」按鈕，按鈕會亮一下。

**食譜卡**
- 學會一道菜時，會出現一張撕邊卷軸樣子的食譜卡：上面是這道菜的手繪圖，下面一行一行列出食材，每行有小圖、族語名稱、中文／英文名稱和「手上有幾個／需要幾個」。
- 還沒見過的食材是灰色的，只看得到族語名稱，名字寫成「???」。
- 找到那樣食材時，那一行就亮起來。也可以去找族語老師，一次把所有食材的名字學起來（要先開始或學會至少一道菜）。

**撿東西只跳小圖**
- 採集、撿拾、觀察時，隊伍上方只會跳出一個小圖示和「+1」，不會打斷遊戲。卡片只在第一次拿到時出現一次。

**圖鑑手札**
- 手札裡的「背包」改成像遊戲道具欄一樣的格子，每格有圖和數量，上面有分類分頁（全部、食材、料理、材料、工具、飾品、服裝、觀察）。
- 點一格，就看到那樣東西的卡片，下面還有原本的說明、營養和做法。服裝一樣可以在這裡穿上或換回。
- 打開手札時，它會從右上角的按鈕長出來；關上時縮回按鈕，大約一秒。

**接到新任務會翻開任務頁**
- 接到新任務時，手札會自動翻到這個任務自己的一頁：任務名稱、目標、提示會很快地一個字一個字打出來，點一下就直接顯示全部。
- 需要的東西用食譜卡的樣子列出來。
- 再點一下任何地方，就關起來。同時接到好幾個任務時，只開一頁，主線任務優先。
- 中英並列時，每段中文下面接一段英文，行數對得起來。

**景點拍照**
- 旅人要在隊伍裡（在附近），第一次走到景點時就會自動拍照：畫面閃一下、有快門聲，洗出來的照片縮小收進相簿，旅人會說一句「📸」開頭的話（每個景點有兩三句，會換著說）。
- 旅人不在隊伍裡時，不會拍、不會存，帶隊的人會說一句「旅人應該會想拍這裡」之類的話。
- 已經拍過的景點，再走過去只會跳一個小相機圖示。
- 需要尊重的地方照原本的規矩：只能遠遠拍的景點就遠遠拍、要先問主人的先問，拍貓頭鷹不用閃光。
- 相簿改成一張張照片排在一起，點一張可以看大圖。
- 現在只要旅人在隊伍裡，誰帶隊都可以拍照，不用先換成旅人。

**舊存檔**
- 舊存檔裡已經學會的詞、已經有的東西，都算已經看過，不會重新跳卡。
- 相簿從空的開始。以前已經完成的拍照任務，帶著旅人再走過去一次，就會補上照片。

**其他**
- 減少動態效果的設定開著時，卡片和手札不飛也不放大，直接淡出。
- 手機直拿、橫拿都檢查過，卡片、任務頁、道具格都放得下。
- 新增一組自動檢查，每次發佈前跑：卡片版面、排隊、飛進按鈕、食譜灰色、族語老師、道具格、任務頁、拍照與相簿、舊存檔。

## English (short)

**0.1.5, phase 1: fixes from play-testing.**

- **Language:** the language buttons are gone from dialogue boxes. You pick a language on the start screens, and you can change it later in the notebook settings next to music and sound. The change applies right away and is remembered.
- **New game:** the male Tao member now leads the party.
- **Camera:** the default view is about 14% farther out. Pinch zoom and reset view both return to the new default.
- **Buildings:** trees in front of buildings and their doors have been cleared, for example at the weather station. An automatic check before every release catches any building that trees hide again.
- **Round-island road:** the road and its shoulders are smoother. No new cliffs and no flooded road.
- **Notices:** notices now sit at the top right with shorter text. Item pickups such as "ingredient +1" appear as small text above the party that fades out, without waiting for other notices.
- **Villagers:** fixed villagers who walked one way along the road forever or got stuck in houses (for example at Ivalino). Villagers now stay near their own spot. Background villagers walk at one third of the player's speed and step rate. Party members still keep pace with the player.
- **Goats:** goats now graze and wander in small herds. Most are black, with a few brown or white.
- **Moored yacht:** the stern of the yacht at the harbour is now white. The navy band runs along the sides and stops just around the stern corners, matching the opening art.
- **Other:** the version number now shows on the title screen and in the notebook settings.

**0.1.5, phase 2: new arrival opening.**

- **Before choosing how to travel:** the same single view of Lanyu as before.
- **By plane:** the camera pulls back to the cabin window and wing; while the island loads the plane rocks gently and clouds drift. Then the camera moves out through the window, the wing goes back past the camera, and the island turns to a new, closer angle. The plane flies out from behind the camera on an arc (from behind, banking, then descending toward Lanyu) with spinning propellers and wingtip trails. The shot fades into the notebook, which stamps "Arrive on Lanyu" complete.
- **By boat:** the camera pulls back to the bow; while loading the waves rise and fall gently. Then the view switches to the yacht seen from behind. The hull rocks on its own, the wake and spray grow as it speeds up, and it shrinks into the centre left near the island while the island turns to the closer angle and grows. Then the same notebook ending.
- **Phones:** the window frame and the bow are never cropped, in portrait or narrow landscape. The loading bar is centred and see-through. The start button on the party screen no longer covers the male/female buttons.
- **Other:** the opening art was re-packed as a new set of about 2 MB; unused old images were removed.

**0.1.5, phase 3: new party screen; the two Tao members may come from different villages.**

- **Party screen:** the three portraits sit side by side in the middle (male Tao member, female Tao member, traveller), each with one line about why they came. One line on top says the three travel together, each with their own purpose. The two Tao members are fixed. Tap the traveller's portrait (or the small male/female buttons) to switch. Each Tao member has a small village dropdown, both set to "random" by default. They may pick the same village or different ones, and "random" draws for each member separately. The whole screen fits on desktop and on phones held upright or sideways, and the start button never covers the portraits or dropdowns.
- **Different villages:** the party stays at the male member's home (the host family). Joint tasks happen there, such as hearing the island's rules, family dinner and meeting at the pavilion. "Coming home" becomes two check-ins: each member visits their own family first (switch members with the portraits at the top left), and then both meet at the host family's pavilion. The elders no longer say both grew up in the same house. Tasks from a woman elder (taro field, weaving, drying fish, cooking) take place at the female member's home. Tasks from a man elder (boat building, going to sea) take place at the male member's home. A task for only one member takes place at that member's home. In "Finding roots", each member gets their clothes from their own family. If an elder you are sent to is your own family's elder, you are sent to an elder in another village instead, so nobody tells you two different stories. Sharing taro with relatives works the same way. When the villages differ, the continue line shows both, for example "Yayo / Ivalino".
- **Old saves:** they still load, with both members from the same village as before.
- **Checks:** before every release, every combination of the six villages is checked. Every quest must find its people and places, and no elder may be visited twice.

**0.1.5, phase 4: a bigger island, level house terraces, a path to every building, the cross-island road, and a travel map.**

- **Bigger island:** the whole island is about 40% bigger. The ground, the round-island road, the villages, the landmarks, quest places, start points, the areas villagers and goats move in, the minimap and the camera limits all grew together. The villages are farther apart; the round-island road went from about 300 m to about 460 m. Imorod is now at its real place, next to Iratay, so those two are still fairly close, but the coast road from Imorod to Ivalino is long, and the new cross-island road is the short way.
- **Houses on terraces:** every house, work house, pavilion, church and school stands on a level terrace cut into the slope, with a short bank on the uphill side. Nothing floats on a slope any more. Houses are farther apart, with at least about 1.3 m between any two, so you can walk between them. Buildings on the sea side of the road now face the road.
- **Paths:** every building has a small path from its door to the road, or to the path of the house next to it. This includes every building a quest sends you to (the shop, the school, the clinic, the airport, the weather station, the lighthouse). The paths are packed earth with flat stones, and gentle to walk. No trees or plants grow on them. Before every release an automatic check confirms that every building has a path, every path reaches the road, nothing blocks it, and no part is too steep. No building is hidden by trees.
- **Cross-island road:** a new road over the hills from Imorod to Ivalino, roughly along the real route, past the weather station at the top. The climb is gentle and the road surface is smooth, and it meets the round-island road without a step. The weather station has a level yard you can walk to from the road.
- **Travel map:** "Round-island travel" now opens a big map of the island. Tap a place to go there: the six villages, the harbour, the airport, the clinic and the Qingqing grassland. The old list of buttons is gone. A red arrow shows where you are. The whole map fits on a computer and on a phone held upright or sideways.
- **Old saves:** 0.1.4 saves still load. Each party member is put on the road near the same village or place.

**0.1.5, phase 5: English version.**

- **Pick English:** the start screens (next to music and sound) and the notebook (Save → Settings) now have "Language: 中文 / English". The choice is remembered. Switching reloads the page once, and progress is saved first. Players who never picked one, and old saves, stay in Chinese as before.
- **Dialogue modes:** the old three-way choice is now called "Dialogue". In English it reads "Tao + English / English only / Tao only".
- **What is in English:** every quest, conversation, item, recipe, Tao word card, song note, milestone and help page — about 3,200 pieces of text — plus the title screen, party screen, arrival choice, buttons, notices, every notebook page, saving and settings.
- **Tao stays Tao:** Tao words are kept (akokey, soli, tatala …), with an English explanation wherever the Chinese gives one. Villages use their Tao names: Yayo, Iraraley, Iranmeylek, Ivalino, Imorod, Iratay. "Teaching draft" and "still needs checking with the community" notes are kept. The word list is in docs/en-glossary.md.
- **It fits:** checked on a computer and on a phone held upright or sideways; no button, tab, list or setting is cut off. On phones the season buttons use short names (Fish-calling, Flying fish …).
- **Place names too** (added after the map work): the place labels floating over the island (villages, the harbour, the airport, the health station, sights like Hongtou Rock), the pins on the travel map and the places quests mention are all in English. Villages use their Tao names, so the extra Tao line under them is gone. Other places follow the word list: Kaiyuan Harbour, Hongtou Rock, Qingqing Prairie … "外婆" is always "Grandma".
- **中文＋English and new fonts:** the language picker has a third choice, "中文＋English"; its buttons are always written in both languages. With no saved choice the game follows the browser: a Chinese browser starts in Chinese, an English or any other browser starts in 中文＋English. In that mode the Chinese in dialogue and notices is a little smaller, with a much smaller English line under it; with one language the sizes stay as before. New font set "霞光書卷": LXGW WenKai TC for Chinese titles, text and the notebook, Cormorant Garamond for English and Tao. Cormorant runs small and thin, so English dialogue, notices and notebook text are a little larger and bolder. The fonts went from about 1.9 MB to about 1 MB. Tao words are never in italics, anywhere. Dialogue and notices had a light pass to sound relaxed but careful; the fixed terms in the word list did not change.
- **Checks:** before every release, an automatic check confirms that every piece of player text (place names included) has English, that no Chinese is left inside the English, and that names and numbers are not lost. Every Chinese interface line in the code must turn into English, an English screen fails if it shows even one Chinese character, and English must fit on all three screen sizes.

**0.1.5, phase 6: cards and the field notebook.**

- **A card the first time:** the first time you learn a Tao word, get an item, learn a dish or finish a set of clothes, a postcard pops up. It has a simple Tao pattern background, a picture on the left, and on the right the Tao name with the Chinese, English or both underneath (following the language setting). Three or more new words at once become one list card. When several cards come at once they wait in line. Tap a card and it shrinks and flies into the notebook button at the top right, which glows.
- **Recipe cards:** learning a dish shows a torn-scroll card with a sketch of the dish and one row per ingredient: a small picture, the Tao name, the Chinese/English name and "have / need". Ingredients you have not seen yet are grey and show only the Tao name, with "???" for the name. A row lights up when you find that ingredient, or the Tao language teacher can teach all the names at once (once you have started or learned at least one dish).
- **Picking things up:** gathering, picking up and observing only show a small picture with "+1" above the party. The card comes only the very first time.
- **Field notebook:** the bag is now a grid of item slots with pictures and counts, with category tabs (all, ingredients, dishes, materials, tools, accessories, clothes, observations). Tap a slot to see its card, plus the old notes, nutrition and steps; clothes can still be put on or taken off there. The notebook grows out of its button when it opens and shrinks back into it when it closes, in about a second.
- **New quests open their page:** accepting a quest opens its own notebook page. The name, goal and hint type out quickly; tap once to show everything. The things you need are listed like a recipe card. Tap anywhere to close. Several quests at once open one page, main quests first. In Chinese + English, each Chinese paragraph is followed by its English one.
- **Photos at scenic spots:** with the traveller in the party and nearby, the first visit to a spot takes a photo by itself: a flash, a shutter sound, the print shrinks into the album, and the traveller says a line starting with "📸" (two or three lines per spot, in turn). Without the traveller nothing is saved, and the leader says the traveller would want a photo here. Later visits only show a small camera icon. Sensitive places keep their rules: distance-only spots, ask the owner first, and no flash for the owl. The album is now a set of prints; tap one to see it large. Anyone can lead for a photo now, as long as the traveller is in the party.
- **Old saves:** words and items you already had count as seen, so no cards replay. The album starts empty; for photo quests finished before this update, walk past the spot again with the traveller to add the photo.
- **Other:** with reduced motion on, cards and the notebook fade instead of flying or growing. Checked on phones held upright or sideways. A new automatic check runs before every release: card layout, the queue, flying into the button, grey recipe rows, the teacher, the item grid, quest pages, photos and the album, and old saves.
